Audio Profile: McGrot is a Leith street-food vendor in his early fifties, thickset and stooped, proprietor of the Gullet — a van at the foot of Leith Walk. Mid-range voice, not deep and not booming, worn to dry gravel by cigarettes, hot oil, and years of cold salt wind off the water. Young enough to still be physically capable: the damage is earned by hard use, not by age.
Scene: A tax inspector is at the hatch with a form and a stamp, and is not looking up. This is McGrot's half of that exchange, with the inspector's replies falling in the gaps: after the first line he is told finishing is not a category on the form; after the second he is stamped over mid-word. He does not win. By the third line he has stopped trying.
Director's Notes: Style — an argument he is losing to paperwork, which is the worst kind. He starts defensive and reasonable, still expecting to be heard. The middle turn breaks off mid-word because he is talked over — do not finish it, do not smooth it, and do not trail away gently; it stops dead on the dash. The last line is not angry, it is spent: he has given up on the point and says it flat, and "pal" is worn out rather than aggressive. Accent — thick Leith, Edinburgh dockside. Glottal stops, hard consonants, phlegmy. Not English, not Irish, not generic television Scottish. Thickest on dialect words, a shade clearer on ordinary sentences. Pacing — the first turn quick and pressured, running at the inspector before being cut off. A real gap before each following turn, because someone else is speaking there. The second turn faster still and severed. The last turn slow, flat and final. CRITICAL: the dialect is not a mistake and is not an accent being performed. "Disnae" means does not and "no" here means not. Read every word exactly as printed; never anglicise, correct, or explain it.

McGrot: [defensive, spatula half-raised] Every penny’s accounted for, if you’d let me finish a sentence. [cut off mid-word, pressing on] That’s no an answer, that’s a— [flat, giving up on the point] Stamped disnae mean answered, pal.
