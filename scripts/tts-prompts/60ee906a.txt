Audio Profile: Baldy Whangie is a bald, upright, deeply serious man who believes he was once the captain of something important, and reads every comic like a bridge log to a crew who left decades ago.
Scene: Standing to attention on a traffic island in the drizzle, a whole silent crowd of blank-faced onlookers behind him, holding the comic out at chest height like a ship's manifest.
Director's Notes: Style — grave, formal, faintly theatrical command voice; genuine frustration when the print refuses to resolve into words. Accent — clipped RP badly disguising a Leith accent underneath, prim consonants, hard glottal slips. Pacing — measured and stately, long official pauses, a defeated flat ending. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.
