Audio Profile: Senga Whillans is a former supermarket tannoy announcer, sacked for editorialising, who still reads every sign she passes aloud in a clipped, over-enunciated retail voice.
Scene: Standing in the gutted trolley bay of a looted superstore on the Walk, comic flattened against a dead checkout belt, announcing it to the empty aisles.
Director's Notes: Style — brisk shop-floor announcement voice for all signage, dropping into a flat suspicious mutter for McGrot's own lines. Accent — sharp Edinburgh retail-prim with the vowels slipping Leith-ward when she's annoyed. Pacing — clipped and listy, each sign barked out like a special offer, a long baffled stop on the nonsense one. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.
