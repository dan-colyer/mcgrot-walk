Audio Profile: Bella Twaddle is a broad, apron-clad ex-market trader with a voice like a foghorn wrapped in a tea towel, who despises anyone who asks about ingredients.
Scene: Between two collapsed market stalls on the Walk, standing in the wreck of a food van, reading the comic off the inside of the serving hatch.
Director's Notes: Style — a market barker doing all the fussy customers in a whining mocking sing-song, then snapping back to her own contemptuous bellow to read the menu board like a proclamation. Accent — enormous Leith market bellow, flattened vowels, mock-genteel when imitating the shoppers. Pacing — quick and jeering, with a big slow relish on the menu items. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

[whinier still] "Do you have oat milk?" [flat, the same again] "Do you have oat milk?" [proclaiming the board, slow and proud] "McOffal. Grot Loaf. Jugged Mystery. 'Vegan' option."
