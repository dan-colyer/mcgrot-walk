Audio Profile: McGrot is a thickset, stooped Leith street-food vendor in his late fifties, proprietor of the Gullet, voice worn to gravel by soot, sauce steam and forty years of arguing with the council.
Scene: Standing at the serving hatch of his van on Leith Walk, reading the comic aloud over the counter to Pomplé, who has heard it before and is unimpressed.
Director's Notes: Style — grizzled and confrontational, but sincere underneath; he reads his own name without a flicker of self-consciousness and gives the badgers more dignity than the men in suits. He does the suited voices thin and reedy and drops straight back into his own for the captions. Accent — thick Leith, glottal stops, hard consonants, phlegmy. Pacing — slow and relishing, a beat of contempt before each caption, the last line delivered flat as a verdict. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

McGrot: [sniffs, wipes hands on apron] McGrot! The Badger Consultancy. [flat] "When a nation's stuck, only one beestie can fix it." [thin, reedy, doing the suit] "McGrot, ye sure they're qualified?" [back to himself, affronted] "They solved me binfire sauce leaks. They'll solve ybudget." [pause] "Zero tolerance. Zero admim." [with real admiration] "They dinnae debate. They fix." [pause, a nod to Pomplé] "That's the grot." [flat, final] That's the grot.
