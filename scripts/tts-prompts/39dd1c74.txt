Audio Profile: Peem Sloan is a gaunt, hollow-cheeked pilgrim who walked to Leith from somewhere he will not name, voice reedy and reverent with a wheeze on every in-breath.
Scene: Kneeling on a folded coat in the lee of a fallen wall, a wee stove going, the comic laid out on a flat stone like scripture while he reads it to the flame.
Director's Notes: Style — devotional and hushed, treating a four-panel cooking joke as sacred text; he gives the monk a quavering ancient croak and McGrot a plain honest mumble, and cannot keep the awe out of the last line. Accent — soft Highland lilt worn thin, with long reverent vowels. Pacing — very slow, weighted silences between panels, the final line almost sung. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

Peem: [reverent, breathy] McGrot and the Monk of the Grot Order. [describing, awed] He's climbin'. Snow on the peaks, staff in hand. [reading the jar on his pack] "GROT PASTE." [long pause] [plain, defeated mumble] "The Grot won't cook, y'holiness..." [quavering ancient croak] "You must... commence like I simmer: with resolve." [very long pause, wheezes] [quiet, dawning] And he stirs. [reading the wee jar] "GROT SAUCE." [triumphant, almost sung] "I've grot the sauce." [pause, eyes closed] ...Magic.
