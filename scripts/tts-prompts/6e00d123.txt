Scene: A silent close of surviving tenement brick, nameplates still bolted on, dog sniffing at the mortar bucket. She works a trowel while murmuring to the stonework.
Director's Notes: Style — soft, confiding murmur, as if letting the walls in on a secret. Accent — Borders, gentle and slow. Pacing — unhurried, small pauses to press mortar into place.
