Erchie: [reads signage, flat] McGrot. [beat] Haggis. Fish. [pause, uneasy] There's nae actual talkin' in this one — just the van, the flag, the dug in its wee hat. [long pause, quieter] Then it goes doon. Right doon, under the close, intae the dark wi' the candles and they hooded yins roond a pot. [whispers] There's a sign doon there and aw. [reads, reverent] "The Heart of Kirkcaldy." [long silence] ...I don't ask what's in the pot. Naebody should ask what's in the pot.
