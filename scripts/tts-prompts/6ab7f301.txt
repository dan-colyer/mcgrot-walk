Audio Profile: Effie Rankine is a retired museum cataloguer who has appointed herself keeper of McGrot ephemera, voice dry and precise as an index card.
Scene: A cramped lock-up stacked with labelled boxes, one bare bulb swinging, rain ticking on a corrugated roof. She holds a laminated diagram up to the light with both hands, reading it like an artefact tag.
Director's Notes: Style — hushed academic recital, treating every label as a matter of grave record. Accent — Morningside-prim, clipped vowels, faint disapproval. Pacing — slow, itemising, a small pause after each label as if filing it. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

Effie: [clears throat primly] McGrot! McGrot's Beret. [pause] Exhibit notes, item by item. "Leith Red Wool Blend — faded by salt air." [beat] "Pomplé Bite Indent, nineteen ninety-eight." [tutting] "Broth-absorbent felt layer — stained with marinade rings." "Secret pocket, emergency bone shards." [raises eyebrow] "Embedded gannet fetther — for fearlessness and peck avoidance." "Steam venting channel." [pause] "Steam venting channel — lets brothfumes escape during cooring." "Steam venting channel," again, [sniffs] they do like to repeat themselves. "Charm knot of Salamander Street — relieved to repel seagulls and tax inspectors." [dry] Quite the dual purpose. "Property of M. Grot — dinnae swap." "Beret stitched by the Stitchwife — warranty void if exposed to French cuisine." "Hangen loop, for hanging beret over cauldron." [squinting] "Lunknae seref." [longer pause, baffled] "Sliight hum." [gives up trying to parse it] And here, in bold, the conservator's note: "Do not wash. Do not ory. Do not question the beret." [sets it down, satisfied] Cataloguing complete.
