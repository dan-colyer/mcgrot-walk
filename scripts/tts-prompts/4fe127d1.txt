Audio Profile: Morag Tannock is a shrunken ex-school-dinner-lady turned street cosmologist, her voice a wheedling nasal whine that rises to evangelical fervour whenever gravy is mentioned.
Scene: Beside a smoking oil-drum brazier on a rain-slick corner of the Walk, a ladle in one hand and the comic pegged to a washing line at eye level.
Director's Notes: Style — a street-preacher demonstration, half science lecture, half sermon; she jabs the ladle at the page for emphasis and does the flat-earthers as a snivelling hooded chorus. Accent — nasal Central Belt, Coatbridge-ish, with pulpit stretches on the vowels. Pacing — brisk lecture rhythm broken by triumphant slow declarations. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

Morag: [ringing out] McGrot — The Sphere of Sauce. [narrating, portentous] "When men doubt the world's bend, a Leith chef must" [pause, stirring] "This is cosmic gravy, brewed frae the binfire." [holding the ladle up, evangelical] "The bubble disnae lie. Observe!" [again, louder, as the crowd shrinks back] "The bubble disnae lie. Observe!" [triumphant] "Aye. That's the sphere. That's grot!" [snivelling, hooded, reading the placard] "Leith flat 4EVA." [horrified squeal] "Sorcery! Ye've measured the curve!" [long pause, then flat and smug] ...Magic.
