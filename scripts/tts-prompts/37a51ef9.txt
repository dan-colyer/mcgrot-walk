Audio Profile: Senga Strang is a bird-boned former newsagent who never once stopped doing her paper round, her voice shrill and shredded from forty years of shouting headlines over traffic.
Scene: Behind the caved-in remains of a news-stand at the foot of the Walk, a damp front page pegged to a bit of fence, wind snapping the corners while she cries it to a queue that isn't there.
Director's Notes: Style — full news-vendor bellow on the headlines, dropping to a snide gossipy mutter for the side columns; she does the American in a honking impression she is enormously pleased with. Accent — sharp Leith newsagent bark with a thin Morningside veneer she cannot hold for more than a sentence. Pacing — punchy front-page rhythm, every item announced like it has just this second come in. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.
