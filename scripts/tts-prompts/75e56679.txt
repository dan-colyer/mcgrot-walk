Audio Profile: Effie Habershon is a birdlike former school dinner lady, all cardigan and clattering ladle, who narrates comics to the pigeons in a voice like a kettle about to whistle.
Scene: A drizzly close off the Walk, corrugated iron rattling somewhere behind her, a battered soup urn steaming at her feet. She unrolls the laminated page with great ceremony.
Director's Notes: Style — hushed, reverent narration that keeps breaking into indignant crowd-voices for the other characters. Accent — soft Morningside-prim vowels curdling into broad Leith when she's quoting the mob. Pacing — slow and stately, sudden sharp bursts on the shouted lines. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

Effie: [clears throat primly] McGrot! The Dystopic Old Town. [reading a sign, hushed] "Welcome to Neo-Auld Edinburgh. Filmset. No entry." [beat, gesturing to the dog] Pomple. [reading more signage, sing-song] "Synth-flavour shots." "Syntel-flavour." [as McGrot, resolute] "Phase one — reclaim ine palate." [muttering] Real broth. No orm. [as the crowd, sharp] "You're off-script!" [as McGrot, weary] "Folk still need the old flavour, even in new ruins." [as the crowd again, shrill] "You're off-script! This is a sterile dystopia — no." [as McGrot, quiet and firm] "Let them script dystopias. I'll ladle hope." [murmuring the tag on the dog's collar] Flavour Resistance Unit. [pause, reverent] McGrot wisdom: even in the future, folk sup the past.
