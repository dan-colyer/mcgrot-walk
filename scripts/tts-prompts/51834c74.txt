Audio Profile: Ishbel Tarbolton is a tiny, birdlike ex-librarian with whistling dentures who recites comics as though they were devotional verse, hands folded, eyes half shut.
Scene: On a folding stool beside a dead pillar box, a tartan blanket over her knees, the comic propped on a music stand made of bent railings.
Director's Notes: Style — reverent recitation, unhurried and dreamlike, as though reading scripture to a congregation of one; every character gets the same beatific hush. Accent — soft Perthshire burr, precise and old-fashioned, dentures whistling on the sibilants. Pacing — very slow, long contemplative pauses between panels, a rising note of wonder at the end. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

Ishbel: [gently, whistling on the S] McGrot — The Endless Pull-Up. [long pause, reverent] "Some men train tae lift iron, Mcar trains te lift himself" [pause] "One pull, one mar..." [softer] "Two...broth stretches bones" [wondering, as the crowd] "Is he training... or ascending" [a small satisfied breath] "Maybe both." [firmer, almost a creed] "Strength's nae reps..." [pause] "It's refusal tae drop." [very quiet, rising in awe] "Somewhere above Leith, McGrot still hangs." [long pause, dentures whistle] ...Magic.
