Audio Profile: Wullie Thrapple is a barrel-chested former sweetie-factory foreman with a throat ruined by boiling sugar fumes, who performs comics from atop an upturned fondant vat.
Scene: A collapsed confectionery works off the Walk, copper pipes still ticking as they cool, a faint burnt-caramel stink in the air. He reads to a semicircle of pigeons.
Director's Notes: Style — grandly theatrical, doing the frightened Frenchman in a wobbling falsetto and then the chorus of little men in a sinister sing-song chant, thoroughly enjoying the menace. Accent — broad Leith with a badly-fumbled French flourish. Pacing — big pauses before each chorus line, building the chant like a threat. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

Wullie: [booming] McGrot! The Grot Loompas. [pause, then a quavering fearful falsetto] "Qu'est-ce que...? Grot Loompas... I must be dreaming too, or may I shouldn't have licked that mysteriosus sauce earlier!" [drops to a low sinister sing-song, slow and menacing] "Grot Loompa doo-pity ding," [pause] "We heard McGrot's in a bit of a swing." [pause, relishing it] "Facing closure, feeling the sting," [full shout] "Now we'll teach him the meaning of hing!" [long pause, satisfied wheeze] ...Magic.
