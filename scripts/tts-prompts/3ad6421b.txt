Audio Profile: Rena Stagg is a jangling, over-caffeinated former café owner with a laugh like a dropped tray, her voice bright and brittle and always about a third too loud.
Scene: Perched on the rim of a dead planter outside a smashed shopfront, reading the comic to a queue of nobody, jabbing at each panel with a bent spoon.
Director's Notes: Style — gleeful, gossipy, thoroughly on McGrot's side; she does the two "You disgust me" lines with mounting relish, and the sound effects at full volume with no warning. Accent — fast, clattering Dundonian, vowels flattened, consonants fired off. Pacing — rapid and chattering, sudden barks on the effects, a sour satisfied drawl on the last line. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence.
