Audio Profile: Hamish Habbie is a stringy ex-abattoir hand who now hawks laminated comics from a shopping trolley, voice pitched somewhere between a corncrake and a dial-up modem.
Scene: A cracked pavement outside a boarded chip shop, a mangy dog snuffling at his feet, seagulls wheeling overhead. He holds the comic up to passersby who are not there.
Director's Notes: Style — clipped, contemptuous delivery, punctuated by sudden barked emphasis on the capitalised lines. Accent — broad Leith docker, glottal stops eating half his consonants. Pacing — fast and sneering, then a hard stop before each shouted line. CRITICAL: the quoted comic text contains misspellings and nonsense words; read every quoted line EXACTLY as printed, pronouncing nonsense phonetically with complete confidence. Never correct or smooth them.

Hamish: [spits] McGrot! The Academic Foodie. [gestures at dog] "There's one type ye cannae feed." [mimicking a prim voice] "Almost rebarative in its post-orglastic officiousness." [scoffing, pointing back] "Almost rebarative in its post-giastic officiousness." [beat] Wordies. [bellowing] EAT IT WITH YER TEETH! [louder still] LESS MASTICATION, MORE DESPERATION! [flat, weary] Some folk hate the idea o food. Wisnae made tae be safe.
