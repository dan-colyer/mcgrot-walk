# The McGrot Canon — cast reference

Authoritative cast table, supplied by Dan at the E3 phase gate (2026-08-07).
The **established / suggested** split binds: *established* facts are canon to
honour; *suggested* details (most physical colours and garments) are design
freedom, standardised here for game consistency. **Disposition** is the E3
phase-gate ruling — where each entry lands in the roadmap, or why it is cut.
The player is the keeper, not McGrot (Direction ruling, `docs/ROADMAP.md`).

The Central Bar test and the LEITH.md sensitivity rules apply to every line
any of these characters speaks. The verbatim rule applies to every comic
fragment any of them quotes.

## Principals

### McGrot — `MCGROT_BASE` → E10a
Rogue Leith food vendor; owner of **the Gullet**. Late 50s–60s; average
height, thick, stooped, work-worn. Weathered pale skin, ruddy nose and
cheeks, sauce and soot marks; small grey-green eyes, heavy bags, suspicious
squint; greasy iron-grey hair swept back; heavy grey stubble. Stained
off-white vest, battered dark apron, old work trousers, heavy dockside coat
in cold scenes; scuffed black work boots. Props: spatula, ladle, sauce
bottle, grease-stained cloth, mystery-meat parcel. Palette: soot black,
dirty cream, rust brown, congealed mustard, dark red. Silhouette: broad
apron shape, hunched shoulders, jutting chin; slow prowl broken by sharp
ladle gestures. Grizzled, confrontational, poetic, stubborn, strangely
sincere. *Established:* role and temperament. *Suggested:* exact colours and
garments. **Disposition: principal NPC and vendor at the Gullet,
date-seeded presence ("McGrot was in the day"). Not the player.**

**Measured against the drawings, 2026-08-16 (G6b.1). The *suggested* half above
is superseded by what follows; the *established* half is not.** Two workers read
156 McGrot-titled comics in disjoint halves and never saw each other's records —
`docs/comic-features-set-a.md` and `-set-b.md`, per-comic IDs in both. Counts
are across ~141 usable appearances. **Distributions are kept rather than
collapsed to one outfit**, on Dan's 2026-08-16 note that the costume should vary
by day (ROADMAP § 12.3).

**Costume reskin is a recurring pattern, not a set of one-offs** — Braveheart
`124327ee`, spacesuit `0da7cd5d`, Starfleet `2ad5f32c`, tactical `2aef315d`,
tapestry `0738152e`, Santa `51256a62`. The beret and the face survive all of
them, which is the direct evidence behind § 12.3.

**The silhouette a stand-in must hit:** beret, round jowly head, stocky low
build with minimal neck. `60ee906a` grafts that head alone onto six unrelated
bodies and every one still reads as McGrot — get those three right and wrong
garments are survivable; get one wrong and correct garments will not save it.

**Where the two halves disagree**, kept rather than smoothed: set-a reads him as
clean-shaven in the large majority, set-b as light stubble. Nobody has adjudicated.

**Corrections to the prose above**, each with the drawings behind it: no beret is
mentioned anywhere in it, yet the beret is the single most consistent feature in
the corpus; "greasy iron-grey hair swept back" is never drawn, the beret covers
the head in every sighting; "stained off-white vest" appears nowhere; the palette
omits green, which is his actual dominant clothing colour.

**Isolated references for modelling:** `417cec3d` head portrait, `090b09a1` and
`36d0f759` full standing figures, `5f1b8828` product sheet, and `66f4b1a4` — an
existing 3D CGI McGrot, worth opening before G8a starts from scratch.

### Pomplé — `POMPLE_BASE` → E10a (shop visits already in E9b)
McGrot's dog and moral compass. Small-to-medium, compact and sturdy; scruffy
dark-charcoal and dirty-cream fur; large brown eyes, tired judgemental
stare; shaggy brow, muzzle and ears. Usually bare; occasional tiny coat,
scarf or comic hat. Props: handwritten sign, small hat, lead, food bowl.
Palette: charcoal, cream, faded red, dull blue. Silhouette: low compact
shape, oversized brows and ears; minimal motion, long stares, tiny head
tilts. Loyal, patient, sceptical, quietly compassionate. *Established:*
dog, moral role, hats and signs. *Suggested:* breed and colouring.
**Disposition: posted at the Gullet; wanders in to the player's shop (E9b,
already written). Hint-system/conscience-meter uses stay unbuilt in the
POC.**

**Measured against the drawings, 2026-08-16 (G6b.1). Supersedes the *suggested*
half above.** He appears in ~122 of the 156 comics read, always inside McGrot's
strips — only two of 418 titles name him.

**The correction that matters: canon says "dark-charcoal and dirty-cream", and
charcoal is never drawn — not once, in either half, independently.** Both
workers reached this separately, which is why it is stated flatly. The palette
line ("charcoal, cream, faded red, dull blue") needs the same revision.

**Where the two halves disagree, and it is unresolved:** set-b finds him
overwhelmingly puppy-proportioned — oversized head and paws on a small body —
while set-a reads a labrador/terrier-cross of ordinary adult proportion. This is
a real split on the single most load-bearing modelling decision for him, and
G6a's stand-in already assumes the adult read. Settle it before G8a builds him.

**The rest of canon holds.** The sign prop, the silent moral-compass role and the
"tired judgemental stare" all match the drawings; the stare is the one place set-b
found the art agreeing with the prose exactly.

### Keth with One Eye — `KETH_ONE_EYE` → E10b (rides E4)
Leith warning figure, keeper of unsettling local knowledge. 50s; tall,
narrow, slightly bent; pale weather-beaten skin with dock grime; one sharp
amber-brown eye, the other covered or scarred; thin dark-grey hair to the
collar; patchy moustache. Long oilskin coat over a faded jumper, old sea
boots. Props: black eye patch, dock hook as walking stick, folded local
maps. Palette: oilskin black, sea green, nicotine yellow, bruise purple.
Still posture, then sudden pointing or leaning. Cryptic, watchful, ominous,
occasionally helpful. *Established:* name, single eye, local-knowledge
role. *Suggested:* appearance. **Disposition: fixed post; warning lines via
E4's comment machinery (the haar, the hinge, the Nether Leithers). The
street's diegetic tutorial.**

### Mike English — `MIKE_ENGLISH` → E10b (rides E4)
Rival van operator from Leith Links who pretends to be English. 40s;
medium-tall, tidy, slightly soft; fair, groomed; light blue eyes,
competitive side-eye; neatly parted light-brown hair. Pressed white shirt,
striped apron, red waistcoat, immaculate trousers, polished brown shoes.
Props: union-themed napkins, silver tongs, menu board, fake accent cue
cards. Palette: cream, burgundy, navy, polished silver. Upright, precise
serving motions collapsing into flustered gestures. Competitive,
theatrical, status-conscious, secretly insecure. *Established:* rival role,
Leith Links origin, false English identity. *Suggested:* appearance.
**Disposition: THE rival (the generic Rival Vendor template is cut). Van
pitched near the Gullet; McGrot–Mike two-actor event joins E4's street
events. LEITH.md incomer rules apply — he can be won over.**

### The Taxman — `TAXMAN` → E10b (rides E4, theatre only)
Bureaucratic adversary after a cut of McGrot's revenue. 40s–50s; thin,
rigid, upright; pale office complexion; flat grey eyes behind rectangular
glasses; severely combed dark hair, receding. Cheap charcoal suit, short
raincoat, narrow tie, practical black shoes. Props: clipboard, calculator,
receipt roll, rubber stamp, evidence bags. Palette: charcoal, beige, paper
white, warning red. Long rectangular silhouette; clipped steps, repetitive
stamping. Literal, persistent, humourless. *Established:* tax-adversary
role. **Disposition: recurring two-actor street event at the Gullet ("the
audit"). The debt-timer mechanic is NOT built in the POC.**

### The Government Inspector — `GOV_INSPECTOR` → E10b (rides E4, theatre only)
Official trying to license, regulate or close the Gullet. High-vis jacket
over office clothes, safety shoes, protective gloves. Props: inspection
tablet, thermometer probe, forms, specimen jar, face mask. Palette:
high-vis yellow, navy, sterile white, hazard orange. Boxy jacket, raised
clipboard; cautious leaning and recoil. Procedural, nervous, determined,
increasingly bewildered. *Established:* inspectors as a recurring type.
**Disposition: "the inspection" street event at the Gullet. The
shutdown-threat mechanic is NOT built in the POC.**

### The Leith Badger — `LEITH_BADGER` → E∞ delight
Wildlife undermining the Gullet. Low, heavy, muscular; coarse black/grey/
dirty-white fur, white facial stripes, big digging claws. Props: mud, a
stolen food wrapper, a scrap of warning tape. Persistent, practical,
territorial, unimpressed. *Established:* badgers undermining the Gullet.
**Disposition: burrow by the Gullet, undermined cobbles, date-seeded
appearances. Prop-cost canon.**

## McGrot variants

- **Exoskeleton McGrot** — `MCGROT_EXOSKELETON`. Base McGrot bulked out with
  cheap online physio devices: knee springs, calf toners, posture harness,
  elbow braces, battery packs, loose cables; neon plastic and hazard red
  over the base palette. *Established:* the cheap-exoskeleton and
  calf-toner concept. **Disposition: date-seeded appearance variant of the
  E10a NPC — dressing, not a movement system.**
- **Ailment-Seeking McGrot** — `MCGROT_AILMENT`. Base McGrot hunting a new
  ailment: mismatched supports, home-made medical labels, symptom notebook,
  neck brace, jaw wrap, beak-teeth diagram, jowl-strain support; medical
  beige and ointment yellow. *Established:* the ailment hunt, beak teeth,
  jowl strain. **Disposition: date-seeded appearance variant, as above.**
- **McWonka** — `MCWONKA_VARIANT`. McGrot's grandiose factory-master dream
  persona: congealed-purple tailcoat, striped waistcoat, meat-cane, tall
  hat, golden ladle. *Established:* the factory concept. **Disposition: CUT
  with the dream world. At most a future date-seeded costume; no dream
  level in the POC.**

## Cut or subsumed

- **Nether Leithers** — `NETHER_LEITHER`. Established hidden community
  beneath Leith (chalk-pale, layered salvage, tunnel lanterns, fish-bone
  charms). **Parked to references: spoken of (Keth, the Street Drinker
  Philosopher), plus one sealed tunnel-door prop. No subterranean location
  in the POC.**
- **Grot Loompa** — `GROT_LOOMPA`. Dream-factory assistant (processed-pink
  sheen, striped smock, miniature cleaver, synchronised marching). **CUT
  with the dream world.**
- **Rival Vendor** — `RIVAL_VENDOR`. Generic competing-trader template.
  **CUT — Mike English is the rival.**
- **Local Customer / Tourist Customer** — `CUSTOMER_LOCAL` /
  `CUSTOMER_TOURIST`. Established customer types. **Subsumed by the
  LEITH.md archetypes** (the Auld Boy, the Posh Students, the Gentrifier
  already are these people, with better lines).
