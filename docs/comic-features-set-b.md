## Part 2 — synthesis

Colour is measured with `scripts/comic-palette.mjs`, run against
`349c1790`, `5f1b8828` and `641002fc` individually (the tool takes one image
per run) plus the whole-418-comic baseline from the same script with no
`--only` filter. **Caveat on what these numbers are worth:** the tool pools
colour across the *whole* frame via k-means: it returns the dominant clusters
of an image, not a colour sampled from one named garment. A beret or a dog's
coat is a small fraction of total pixels and will not surface as its own
cluster next to a large background. So the palette runs below are read as
corroboration of a *palette family* (warm, desaturated, olive/mustard/rust),
not as a source for a specific "the beret is `#RRGGBB`" claim — no hex in this
document was measured off an isolated garment, because the tool has no
region-of-interest mode. Whole-corpus baseline: mean luma 0.473, mean
saturation 0.455, with the two largest clusters at `#6d6428` (12.5% share) and
`#4d4627` (11.3%) — both desaturated olive-browns. The three single-image runs
land in the same family: `349c1790` peaks at `#615c40`/`#eac690`/`#dbb07d`
(olive, cream, tan); `5f1b8828` (a flatter, more saturated poster) peaks at
`#c38f2b`/`#0f2e0a`/`#e4cc70` (mustard-orange, dark green, pale gold);
`641002fc` (a darker night scene) peaks at `#3e3215`/`#4b3917` (near-black
browns). None of the three surfaces a bright red as a dominant cluster even
where a beret is on-screen — consistent with the beret being small in frame,
not with the beret being a different colour than it looks.

### McGrot

**Stable** (present in the clear majority of this set's 70 appearances):
- **Round, bald or near-bald head with a jowly, stubbled lower face** — present
  in every clear appearance including the isolated head-only references
  (417cec3d's portrait, 2e075331's diagram, and 60ee906a's parody where the
  head alone, grafted onto other bodies, still reads as "McGrot").
- **Red/orange-red beret** — present in an estimated 66 of the 70 clear
  appearances. Swapped for other headwear in a handful of costumed or
  differently-styled strips: green military garrison cap (558182ae, "Boot
  Camp"), white sun/bucket hat (5eeec173, "The Wiimblendon Wobble"), green knit
  beanie (5576df0c, "The New Year Error"), no headwear at all in the one
  naturalistically-rendered appearance (37d5a9ae).
- **Green shirt, often tartan/plaid-patterned** — the default top in the large
  majority of appearances (e.g. 2d9e0cd9, 303358c8, 32686cc7, 349c1790,
  3215a69a, 39dd1c74, 5eeec173, 641002fc). A number of night-palette strips
  (378a560c, 50f0b324, 51834c74, 61a16f6a) read the shirt as very dark/near-
  black; given every one of those is a low-light/night scene and no
  cool-lit comic shows a genuinely different-coloured shirt, this reads as
  lighting rather than a real wardrobe variant.
- **Stocky, thick-set, short-limbed build** — universal across every
  appearance with a visible body.
- **Ladle or spoon prop** — present in the clear majority of cooking-context
  panels (2d9e0cd9, 315d8c0e, 349c1790, 3e0d6700, 4fe127d1, 62e342f1, 6075cdd0,
  comic1, comic3, and many more).

**Variant** (recurs, but changes):
- **Lower garment**: green dungarees/overalls are the most common choice
  (2db7525f, 3215a69a, 3ad6421b, 3e6e85cd, 4fe127d1, 641002fc, 69ef9026), but
  blue dungarees appear once (38edcb41), a tartan kilt appears once
  (3de3b7ca), and cream/tan trousers appear once in the dream sequence
  (4f601044). **This contradicts `docs/CANON.md`'s "old work trousers" as the
  stable read** — see Contradictions below.
- **Apron**: a distinct, separately-coloured apron layer (as opposed to the
  shirt/dungarees alone) appears in only a minority of this set — explicitly
  named "Apron of resistance (gravy)" in 3e0d6700, and visible as a darker
  overlay in 5b20ab78 and 349c1790. In most appearances there is no separate
  apron drawn at all; the plaid shirt tucked into dungarees does the work
  canon assigns to an apron.
- **Facial hair**: clean-shaven/light stubble in the large majority, but a
  full beard appears in 3f75d6be ("Operation Aqua Cross").
- **Costume one-offs**: full Santa suit (51256a62), full military uniform
  (558182ae), formal cardigan and bow-tie in the McWonka-adjacent dream
  sequence (4f601044).

**One-off:**
- A 3D-CGI-rendered McGrot exists in the corpus (66f4b1a4, "The Organoid
  Engine") — the only non-flat-vector treatment of the character found in this
  set, and directly relevant to any 3D-modelling phase as an existing attempt.
- A full-body toy/figure design (36d0f759) and a busy-board product sheet
  (5f1b8828) both give clean, isolated full-body silhouettes without a scene
  around them — useful modelling references precisely because nothing else is
  competing for attention in the frame.
- A single framed portrait (417cec3d) is the cleanest head-only reference
  found: round face, furrowed brow, beret, in isolation.

**Silhouette.** At range, McGrot reads as a stocky, thick-set figure with short
limbs and a low centre of gravity, topped by a small rounded head wearing a
flat-sided orange-red beret. The beret-plus-round-head pairing alone is
sufficient for recognition — proven by 60ee906a, where the parody grafts just
that head shape onto six unrelated Star Trek bodies and every one of them
still reads unmistakably as "a McGrot." A 3D stand-in that gets the beret
silhouette, the round jowly head, and the stocky low build right will read as
McGrot even if every other garment detail is wrong; get any of those three
wrong and the rest won't save it.

**The head.** The single most-drawn feature set is: round face, bald or
near-bald scalp, heavy brow often furrowed into a frown, small dark eyes,
jowly/fleshy lower face, light stubble, all under the red beret. It is drawn
consistently enough, across enough unrelated art styles (flat vector, 3D CGI,
toy-figure, framed portrait, meme-format grafting), that it functions as the
character's signature far more than any single garment does.

### Pomplé

**Stable** (present in 60 of 78 comics in this set):
- **Small-to-medium dog, floppy ears, rounded snout** — the default build in
  the clear majority of appearances.
- **Cream/tan/gold coat colour** — this is the single strongest, most
  consistent finding for Pomplé, and it **contradicts `docs/CANON.md`**: see
  below.
- **Handwritten sign/placard prop, worn by or placed beside the dog** — a
  recurring gag across unrelated strips: "CAKE COMPLIANCE" (2d5c1cef),
  "NARRATIVE COMPLIANCE UNIT" (37d5a9ae), "GUARD DOG" (58e960b3), "ASK ME
  ABOUT GROT SAUCE" (3de3b7ca). This one genuinely matches canon's "props:
  handwritten sign" almost exactly.
- **Mostly silent/reactive** — Pomplé rarely speaks; he watches, reacts, and
  is occasionally addressed by name (56c5b40c, 3e3a7fd4, 5950b4db, 636d4903,
  comic1).

**Variant:**
- **Size/age**: overwhelmingly drawn puppy-proportioned (oversized head/paws,
  small body) rather than as a mature adult dog. A handful of strips draw him
  visibly larger and more adult: 5a3017e2 ("Pomplé in the Steamlands" — the
  one strip that is actually about him by name) and 53088e97/5494ce23, both
  also darker brown/chocolate rather than tan.
- **Coat texture/colour outliers**: white and notably fluffier in 3f75d6be
  (worn with sunglasses) and 51256a62 (Santa hat); white/cream and more
  upright/terrier-like by the end of 558182ae's boot-camp strip.
- **Accessories**: scarf matching McGrot's own (3e3a7fd4), knit beanie/bonnet
  (641002fc), orange safety vest (57d79392), red guide-dog-style harness
  (5d49e093), spa robe (5a3017e2).

**One-off:**
- A doppelganger "mirror" dog wearing a human "VEGAN" cap and speaking a full
  line (3ad6421b) — thematically a mirror-universe antagonist, not treated
  here as a genuine Pomplé data point.
- A toy/figure design (36d0f759) shows him seated in/behind a bucket-like
  shape on a wheeled cart — useful as an isolated full-body silhouette
  despite the odd prop.

**Silhouette.** Low, compact, four-legged, most often at knee height beside
McGrot, with oversized floppy ears and a puppy-proportioned head-to-body
ratio. He is drawn small enough, and static enough, that his silhouette reads
mainly through posture (sitting, watching) rather than motion.

**The head.** Round snout, large dark eyes, floppy ears, and — when the strip
gives him an expression at all — a flat, faintly disapproving look rather than
an eager one, which is the one place the art consistently matches canon's
"tired judgemental stare."

### Where the drawings contradict `docs/CANON.md`

- **Canon, Pomplé:** *"Small-to-medium, compact and sturdy; scruffy
  dark-charcoal and dirty-cream fur."* The drawings disagree on the primary
  colour: across every clear appearance in this set, Pomplé is cream, tan or
  gold — occasionally white (3f75d6be, 51256a62, 558182ae) or chocolate-brown
  (5a3017e2, 53088e97, 5494ce23) — but not once drawn as dark-charcoal. Since
  canon already marks colouring as *"Suggested"*, this is not a canon breach so
  much as evidence the suggested default was never actually realised in the
  art and should probably be revised to match what's drawn — e.g. compare 60
  or so tan/gold/cream appearances against zero charcoal ones.
- **Canon, McGrot:** *"old work trousers... battered dark apron."* The
  drawings more often show green dungarees/overalls doing double duty as both
  trousers and upper covering, with no separate apron layer at all (e.g.
  2db7525f, 3215a69a, 349c1790, 3e6e85cd) — a distinct apron garment is the
  minority case (3e0d6700, 5b20ab78), not, as canon implies, the default.
  Trouser colour also varies once each to blue (38edcb41) and a kilt
  (3de3b7ca), against canon's single "old work trousers" line.
- **Canon disposition, McWonka/dream-factory material:** marked *"CUT with the
  dream world... no dream level in the POC."* Regardless of that build
  decision, the source comics do draw this material: 4f601044 shows an actual
  McWonka-adjacent dream encounter with four Grot Loompas, and 69b064db shows
  "McGrot vs. Grot Loompas" as a fight scene. The two also disagree with each
  other on what a "Grot Loompa" looks like — orange-skinned with swept-up
  green hair in 4f601044 and the packaging photo 3f24447e, versus small
  McGrot-faced clones in 69b064db — so if this material is ever revisited, the
  source art itself is not settled on the design.
- **Not in canon at all:** a newborn baby handed to McGrot (6aa1c186, "Baby
  Arrival Surprise") — no offspring or family life is mentioned anywhere in
  `docs/CANON.md`'s McGrot entry. Read here as a one-off gag rather than
  standing lore, but flagged since it's the kind of detail a later session
  might otherwise assume was invented fresh.

### Corpus note (not a canon contradiction, but relevant to anyone counting)

Two exact-duplicate pairs exist under different IDs in this set:
`50f0b324`/`51834c74` (same "Endless Pull-up" strip, two different colour
grades) and `3706c73f`/`5acf1728` (identical "Birth of a Battle Jacket," pixel-
for-pixel same colours). Anyone tallying "of 418" for statistics should know
at least these two pairs double-count a single comic.
