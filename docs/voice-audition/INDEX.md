# McGrot's voice — what to listen to, and for what

**It's down to two: Google's "Algenib" voice and Google's "Orus" voice.**
Both cover all twelve of McGrot's lines plus the argument with the tax
inspector, so this is a genuine side-by-side, not one candidate heard once
and the other heard a dozen times. Group 1 below is the whole decision.
Everything after it is supporting material, not a third or fourth option.

## What to listen for

Six things, and a candidate only works if all six are true. In plain terms:

1. **It's one man throughout.** Not a slightly different voice from line to
   line — the same person every time.
2. **"Naw." sounds like a whole sentence**, not like it got cut off partway
   through something longer.
3. **The accent sounds real**, not like someone doing a Scottish accent for a
   part. "Ken", "shan", "doon the Walk" should sound like his ordinary way of
   talking, not a performance.
4. **He sounds different when he means something.** Play the Pomplé line
   next to the Taxman line — if they sound the same, that's a problem.
5. **The nonsense lines still sound confident.** Some of what McGrot reads is
   deliberately garbled comic-book nonsense, and it has to sound like a man
   who means every word of it, not like the voice stumbling over gibberish.
6. **You could listen to this two hundred times and not get sick of it.**
   Players meet McGrot again and again. Charming once but grating on the
   fifth listen is a fail. This one only you can judge.
