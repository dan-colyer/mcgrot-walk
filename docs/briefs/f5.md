## The fault

`?page=on` paints cream paper, the panel rule and the caption, and the 3D scene
inside the panel is absent. Confirmed as a product fault, not a capture artefact
— reproduced outside Codex's rig at two anchors:

```bash
node scripts/mcgrots-shot.mjs --body=skinned --archetype=rab --page=on --anchor=counter --frames=600 --shot=/tmp/s4-counter.png
node scripts/mcgrots-shot.mjs --body=skinned --archetype=rab --page=on --anchor=far --frames=600 --shot=/tmp/s4-far.png
```

Both give an empty panel. The S4 row of every sheet under
`docs/smoke/captures/mcgrots/g2/` shows the same.

## Why the gates did not catch it

Both existing S4 checks were green throughout:

| gate | reads | what it misses |
|---|---|---|
| S4 insets the render into a panel | panel is 70.9% of the window | the panel's geometry, not its contents |
| S4 renders at the panel size | buffer 1190x549 in window 1280x720 | the buffer's dimensions, not its pixels |

This is the second candidate this session to render nothing while its numeric
gates passed — F4 was a solid black actor through a full suite of green style
checks. Whatever you add must look **inside** the panel.

## A starting point, not a diagnosis

`createPage()` builds a `#page` root and appends it to `document.body`, which
puts it after the canvas in document order, and `.page-paper` appears to be a
full-bleed element. The obvious hypothesis is that the paper covers the canvas
the panel is supposed to reveal. **Measure it; do not agree with me** — the last
two briefs both named the wrong suspect and the measurement found the real one.

`page.js` insets the canvas by setting `style.left/top/width/height` rather than
resizing the renderer. Check what that does to the canvas's stacking context
before assuming plain z-order.
