## The fault

From `docs/MCGROTS-ROADMAP.md` § 10 fault F3: everything in the G1 animation
bake-off was measured on **`rab` alone**. `rab` is the only archetype with a
`-rig.json` sidecar; the other six 404, so anything that drives the game can
only ever see `rab`. That blocks measuring the cast, not just covering it.

The cast varies enough that this matters: the adaptive neck lands between 0.75
and 0.89, and `runt`'s head is 1,055 triangles against `slab`'s 232.

## What exists

`scripts/rig-glb.mjs` already takes the whole biped list —
`kenneth, mcgrot, morag, rab, runt, slab`. `node scripts/rig-glb.mjs` runs all
of them; `node scripts/rig-glb.mjs rab` runs one. It places bones by measurement
and computes skin weights by distance to the nearest bone segment, writing
`assets/characters/<name>-rig.json`.

`rab-rig.json` is the worked example and the only one currently on disk. **Do
not regenerate `rab` unless you have a reason** — it is the baseline every G1
measurement was taken against, and changing it silently invalidates them.

**Pomplé is excluded by design.** He is a quadruped, not a biped, and G6 gives
him his own treatment. He is not in `BIPEDS` — leave it that way.

## The actual work, which is not running the script

Running it is one command. The work is judging what comes out.

The script's own header says it plainly: this is the crude end of auto-skinning
— proper tools solve a heat equation over the surface — and **on lumpy
generated topology it can tear**. A tear is a RESULT, not a failure of the task.
The roadmap time-boxes this candidate and records that a measured rejection
counts as delivered work. So do not tune the rigger to hide a tear; report it.

For each of the five archetypes you generate:

1. Generate the rig.
2. **Render it and open the picture.** `scripts/mcgrots-bakeoff.mjs` renders a
   strip of eight frames across one full stride, which is what a gait has to be
   read off — a still cannot show it. `node scripts/mcgrots-shot.mjs
   --body=skinned --archetype=<name> --anchor=counter --frames=600
   --shot=/tmp/<name>.png` is the cheap single-frame version.
3. Say what you saw, per archetype: does the knee bend the right way, does the
   coat bind to the legs, does the mesh tear at the shoulder or hip, does the
   neck land somewhere sane.

A numeric gate cannot see a bad picture. Every fault found in G2 was found by
opening an image after the numbers said green.

## Definition of done

- Five new `-rig.json` sidecars on disk (`rab` untouched unless justified).
- A per-archetype verdict, each backed by a capture you opened.
- `npm run bundle:mcgrots && npm run smoke:mcgrots` — paste the actual tail.
  25/25 is the current state; if a check goes red, that is a finding, report it
  rather than working around it.
- Landed per `AGENTS.md` § Landing: the sidecars, `docs/MCGROTS-ROADMAP.md`
  updated (F3's status, and any archetype the rigger cannot handle), and a
  commit message carrying what you actually measured.
- **Commit; do not push, deploy or publish.** Those are Dan's call alone.
